Notes
- corro-no-gui.gz is the default-feature terminal build (same binary as
  corro.gz, kept under both names for continuity); gcorro.gz adds the GTK
  GUI backend (GTK itself is runtime-dlopen, never linked).
- The Win95 zips are built locally (rust9x toolchain + VC6 libs + zig are
  not available on CI runners); see scripts/package-win95-release.sh.
  Inner names: corro.exe (pancurses TUI) and gcorro.exe (GUI, selected by
  argv[0] dispatch). unicows.dll is NOT bundled (licensing); the zips
  carry a README95.txt pointing at Microsoft's unicows redistributable.
